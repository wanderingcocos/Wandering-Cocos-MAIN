Do not replace the homepage with a new implementation.

Do not change routing or page structure.

Design Direction

The overall feeling should be:

Editorial
Premium
Minimal
Timeless
Calm
Quiet
Confident

Avoid modern startup aesthetics.

Avoid glassmorphism.

Avoid oversized call-to-actions.

Avoid unnecessary visual effects.

The homepage should feel like the opening spread of a beautifully designed travel journal where photography tells the story and typography quietly supports it. The objective is not to push visitors toward a sale immediately, but to invite them into the world of Wandering Cocos.
