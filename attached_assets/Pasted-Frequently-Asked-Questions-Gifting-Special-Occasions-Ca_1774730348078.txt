Frequently Asked Questions
🎁 Gifting & Special Occasions
Can I send a "Wandering Box" as a gift?
Absolutely! Our boxes are designed for gifting, featuring premium kraft paper tied with a signature ribbon. Just provide the recipient’s name, WhatsApp number, and delivery address during your pre-order.

Can I include a personalized note?
Yes. We can include a handwritten note inside the box to make your gift extra special. Please share your message with us on WhatsApp when you place the order.

How does payment work for gifts?
As the sender, you will complete the UPI payment to confirm the gift delivery. We will coordinate the delivery directly with the recipient to ensure a seamless surprise.

📦 Bulk & Corporate Orders
Do you handle bulk orders for events or offices?
Yes, Wandering Cocos accepts bulk orders for corporate events, birthdays, or gatherings. We are equipped to handle larger volumes as we grow toward our physical cafe.

What is the lead time for bulk orders?
For orders exceeding 5 boxes, please contact us at least 5 days in advance to ensure ingredient sourcing and kitchen availability.

Can I customize a bulk order?
While our weekly subscription boxes are typically fixed, we can discuss custom menu selections or specific branding needs for large-scale bulk orders via WhatsApp.

🥐 Ordering & Delivery
How do I place an order?
We operate on a pre-order basis. Browse our current menu and click the "Order via WhatsApp" button to check availability and receive payment details.

Is there a delivery fee?

HSR Layout (Ambalipura): We offer Free Delivery within a 7 km radius of our HSR kitchen.

Rest of Bengaluru: For addresses outside the 7 km zone, a standard delivery fee applies based on the distance from HSR.

What happens if my delivery is delayed?
We use reliable third-party delivery partners. While we dispatch from HSR strictly on time, unforeseen factors like Bangalore traffic may cause minor delays. Please note that the LLP is not liable for "unfortunate situations" caused by third-party couriers once the box leaves our care.

🛡️ Safety & Policies
Do you cater to specific allergies?
Our kitchen handles wheat, nuts, dairy, and eggs. While we maintain high hygiene standards, we cannot guarantee a 100% allergen-free environment. Please inform us of any severe allergies before ordering.

What is your cancellation policy?
Since we bake specifically for your pre-order, cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled delivery are non-refundable.
