This Privacy Policy outlines how Wandering Cocos collects, uses, and protects your information as we grow from a subscription-based pre-order service into a physical bakery cafe. Since we currently operate via WhatsApp and UPI, this policy is designed to be transparent about our manual and digital data handling.

1. Information We Collect
Because our current model relies on direct communication, we collect the following "Personal Identifiable Information" (PII) when you place a pre-order:

Contact Details: Your name and WhatsApp phone number.

Delivery Information: Your physical home or office address.

Payment Records: Transaction screenshots and UPI IDs used to confirm your order.

Preferences: Information regarding your choice of toppings (e.g., Pistachio vs. Coconut cream) and any noted allergies.

2. How We Use Your Data
We use your information strictly for operational purposes to ensure your "Wandering Box" arrives fresh and on time:

Order Fulfillment: To bake the correct quantity and deliver to the right location.

Communication: To send order confirmations, payment acknowledgments, and delivery ETAs via WhatsApp.

Business Records: To maintain the legal and financial records required for our LLP.

Future Updates: We may occasionally notify you about new menu launches or the opening of our physical cafe, unless you ask us not to.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We value your privacy and do not sell your data to third-party marketers. However, we share necessary details with:

Delivery Partners: Your name, address, and phone number are shared with our delivery personnel or third-party courier services to facilitate drop-off.

Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights and safety of our LLP and its owners.

4. Security of Manual Transactions
Since you are currently paying via personal UPI and sharing data over WhatsApp, please note:

WhatsApp Security: While WhatsApp uses end-to-end encryption, you are responsible for the security of your own device and account.

Payment Data: We do not store your bank account details. We only retain the confirmation of payment (screenshots) for accounting purposes within the LLP.

5. Perishable Goods & Health Data
Allergy Information: Any health-related data you provide (e.g., nut allergies) is treated with high sensitivity and is used solely to ensure your safety during food preparation.

6. Your Rights
You have the right to:

Access: Ask us what data we have stored for you.

Correct: Update your delivery address or contact details.

Delete: Request that we delete your contact information from our records after your order is fulfilled (noting that we must keep financial records for tax purposes).

7. Changes to This Policy
As Wandering Cocos transitions to a website-based checkout or a physical cafe, this policy will be updated to reflect new data collection methods (such as cookies or email marketing). We recommend checking this page periodically.

Contact Us
If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us directly on WhatsApp.
